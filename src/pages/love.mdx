**💎 In a partner, I value:**

- **Curiosity and openness to the unusual** — something in the world lights you up, and "want to try this weird thing?" usually gets a yes
- **Ability to <button class="term" popovertarget="def-unmask">unmask</button><span popover="auto" id="def-unmask" class="term-def">Masking is the constant background effort of performing "normal" — managing eye contact, tone, filtering the weird thought before it lands. - **Willingness to work through hard stuff** — when something's off, we name it kindly instead of letting it fester
- **Some overlap in how we want the world to be** — you don't have to be an organizer, but "that's just how things are" isn't your resting stance
- **Shared interest in community and connection** — you want people in our lives, not just us in a bubble

### 💜 Relationship style notes

- I practice kitchen table poly when possible. I love it when my people like each other!
- I'm looking for a nesting partner (AKA "primary", but I'm generally non-hierarchical), but would start any relationship casually to see if we get along as friends first.
- **I really value stable connection, which takes many forms:** hedonistic sex, intimate friendship, <button class="term" popovertarget="def-comet">comets</button><span popover="auto" id="def-comet" class="term-def">Someone who swings back around every so often — months or years apart — and it's instantly warm and real again. Named for the orbit, not the crash.</span>, primaries, and nesting partners.
- **I want deep partnership alongside, not instead of, each of us having our own communities, projects, and inner lives.** Anchor-partner energy is appealing; sole-source-of-everything energy is not.
- **I come and go in waves** — bursty travel, then home time. I show up fully when I'm here; I trust my people to do their own thing when I'm not.
- Clothing optional when it's just us at home.
- For more on how I show and receive care, see [💌 Love languages](#love-languages).

<h2 id="not-a-match">⚖️ We're not a good match if</h2>

- **You want monogamy.** I have established partners and that's not changing.
- **You're far away and there's no realistic path to closing the distance.** I'm Bay Area-based and open to relocating for the right partner — but I do need us to land in the same place eventually.
- **You need a lot of texting or constant contact.** I hermit-mode for days at a time to recharge; my texting is mostly functional. If you need steady daily reassurance through the phone, we'll both end up frustrated.
- **You want a relationship where partners are each other's everything.** I love deep, anchored partnership — and I do better when we each keep our own communities and inner lives alongside it.
- **You're not interested in intentional community as part of your life.**
