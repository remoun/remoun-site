### 💜 Relationship style notes

- I practice kitchen table poly when possible. I love it when my people like each other!
- I'm mostly looking for a nesting partner (AKA "primary", but I'm generally non-hierarchical), but would start any relationship casually to see if we get along as friends first.
- **I really value stable connection, which takes many forms:** hedonistic sex, intimate friendship, comets, primaries, and nesting partners.
- **I want deep partnership alongside, not instead of, each of us having our own communities, projects, and inner lives.** Anchor-partner energy is appealing; sole-source-of-everything energy is not.
- **I come and go in waves** — bursty travel, solo recharging in between. I show up fully when I'm here; I trust my people to do their own thing when I'm not.
- Clothing optional when it's just us at home.
- For more on how I show and receive care, see [💌 Love languages](#love-languages).

<div class="note-box">
  **👶 Parenting:** I want to be a parent, ideally with my nesting partner. Open to partners who already have kids — and if I'm helping raise children, I'd like at least one to be my biological child.

<h2 id="not-a-match">⚖️ We're not a good match if</h2>

- **You want monogamy.** I have established partners and that's not changing.
- **You're far away and there's no realistic path to closing the distance.** I'm Bay Area-based and open to relocating for the right partner — but I do need us to land in the same place eventually.
- **You need a lot of texting or constant contact.** I hermit-mode for days at a time to recharge; my texting is mostly functional. If you need steady daily reassurance through the phone, we'll both end up frustrated.
- **You want a relationship where partners are each other's everything.** I love deep, anchored partnership — and I do better when we each keep our own communities and inner lives alongside it.
- **You're not interested in intentional community as part of your life.**
